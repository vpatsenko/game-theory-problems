\textbf{Meeting in mountains.}
Two hiking groups (the Red and the Blue) choose where to cross the mountain ridge:
through the Northern pass or through the Southern pass.
They start from the different sides of the ridge and do not have any connection to each other.
If they both choose the Northern pass, then they meet at the pass with probability \(90\%\).
If they both choose the Southern pass, then they meet with probability \(70\%\).
If they choose different passes, they do not meet at all.
The Red get \(10\) units if they meet, and the Blue get \(20\) units in this case.
For the Red, the cost of going through the Northern pass is \(5\) and the cost of going through the Southern pass is \(7\).
For the Blue, the costs are \(10\) for the Northern pass and \(8\) for the Southern pass.
Draw the matrices and solve the game.
(The players are interested only in the expected profit.)

\textbf{Solution.}

Payoff matrix:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Blue }N & \text{Blue }S\\
\hline
\text{Red }N & (10,20) & (0,0)\\
\text{Red }S & (0,0) & (10,20)
\end{array}
\]

\centerline{after applying probabilities}
\[\downarrow\]

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Blue }N & \text{Blue }S\\
\hline
\text{Red }N & (9,18) & (0,0)\\
\text{Red }S & (0,0) & (7,14)
\end{array}
\]

\centerline{after applying the costs}
\[\downarrow\]

\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Blue }N & \text{Blue }S\\
\hline
\text{Red }N & (4,8) & (-5,-8)\\
\text{Red }S & (-7,-10) & (0,6)
\end{array}
\]

\textbf{Pure Nash equilibriums.}

\[
(N,N), \qquad (S,S)
\]

\textbf{Mixed Nash equilibrium.}\mbox{}\\
\noindent Let
\[
p=\mathbb{P}(\text{Red plays }N), \qquad
q=\mathbb{P}(\text{Blue plays }N)
\]


\textbf{Red indifferent.}

\noindent Red's expected payoff from playing \(N\) is
\[
u_R(N)=4q+(-5)(1-q)=9q-5
\]

\noindent Red's expected payoff from playing \(S\) is
\[
u_R(S)=(-7)q+0\cdot(1-q)=-7q
\]

\[
9q-5=-7q
\]
Hence
\[
16q=5,
\qquad
q=\frac{5}{16}
\]

\textbf{Blue indifferent.}

Blue's expected payoff from playing \(N\) is
\[
u_B(N)=8p+(-10)(1-p)=18p-10
\]

Blue's expected payoff from playing \(S\) is
\[
u_B(S)=(-8)p+6(1-p)=6-14p
\]

Set them equal:
\[
18p-10=6-14p
\]
Hence
\[
32p=16,
\qquad
p=\frac{1}{2}
\]

Therefore the mixed Nash equilibrium is
\[
p=\frac{1}{2}, \qquad q=\frac{5}{16}
\]

So, Red plays \(N\) with probability \(\frac{1}{2}\) and Blue plays \(N\) with probability \(\frac{5}{16}\)
