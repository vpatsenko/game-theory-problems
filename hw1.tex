\textit{Assignment.} In two $2\times 2$ games, find all pure and mixed Nash equilibria.

\subsection*{1. Meeting in mountains}

\paragraph{Task.}
\textit{(Meeting in mountains.)} Two hiking groups (the Red and the Blue) choose where
to cross the mountain ridge: through the Northern pass or through the Southern pass.
They start from opposite sides of the ridge and have no way to coordinate.
If both choose the Northern pass, they meet there with probability $90\%$.
If both choose the Southern pass, they meet with probability $70\%$.
If they choose different passes, they do not meet.
The Red receives $10$ units if they meet; the Blue receives $20$ units in that case.
For the Red, crossing the Northern pass costs $5$ and the Southern pass costs $7$.
For the Blue, the costs are $10$ (North) and $8$ (South).
Draw the payoff matrices and solve the game; players care only about expected profit.
